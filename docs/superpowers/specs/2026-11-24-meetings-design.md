# Wren Meetings — design

Date: 2026-11-24. Status: approved by Maximilian (2026-11-24).
Research behind it: `docs/research/granola-teardown.md`, `docs/research/notion-meeting-notes-teardown.md`.

## 1. Intent

Maximilian sits in Microsoft Teams meetings on his work account. He wants Wren to turn each meeting into
**a detailed-yet-simple summary of everything that happened**, one page or a few, without him writing it up.

- During the meeting he watches a **live transcript with real names** (what he liked in Notion), and can jot
  his own rough notes.
- After the meeting he reads **enhanced notes** written by Claude from the transcript plus his notes (what he
  liked in Granola), in **Short, Medium or Long** versions.
- It ships as a polished update to **both** the Windows and macOS Wren apps.

## 2. Goals, non-goals, anti-goals

**Goals**
1. One click to start notes for a Teams meeting; Pause/Resume; Stop.
2. Live transcript: who said what, in order, a few seconds behind speech, names from Teams.
3. A "My notes" editor he can type in during and after the meeting.
4. Enhanced notes in three lengths, generated by Claude with his Anthropic API key.
5. A Meetings page listing past meetings; open any to read its notes, transcript and (for 24 h) audio.
6. Works on Windows (RTX 3060 Ti, CUDA) and macOS (M2, 8 GB, macOS 26.4) with the same look and behaviour.

**Non-goals (decided — do not build)**
- Automatic "meeting detected" prompts or auto-start. **Start is manual only** (Maximilian, 2026-11-24).
- Zoom, Google Meet, Teams-in-a-browser, in-person meetings. **Teams desktop app only.**
- Calendar integration, sharing integrations (Slack/email/Notion export), "chat with this meeting".
- Cloud transcription. Voice-based speaker diarization models.
- A new floating window during meetings (the tray shows recording state instead; see §6.4).
- Any "this meeting is being transcribed" notice to other participants (Maximilian, 2026-11-24: he handles
  that himself).

**Anti-goals (would count as failure even if it "works")**
- Dictation gets slower or less reliable because a meeting is running.
- Silent gaps: audio lost or a segment dropped without the transcript showing it.
- Enhanced notes that invent things not said, or misattribute who said or owns something with confidence.
- The API key appearing in logs, settings.json, the database, or the frontend after it is saved.
- Wren stealing focus from Teams.

## 3. Constraints

- Builds on current `main` (v0.1.3 + macOS port): Tauri 2, React 19, Rust, one Python sidecar per OS
  (Windows `faster-whisper` `large-v3-turbo` on CUDA; macOS `mlx-whisper` `large-v3-turbo-q4`, ~452 MB).
  **The meeting feature reuses that one sidecar and model** — no second model is loaded (the Mac has 8 GB).
- Existing design system (`src/styles/tokens.css` etc.), Light/Dark/System themes, sidebar layout.
- Teams: new Teams desktop client (`ms-teams.exe` on Windows; "Microsoft Teams" on macOS), work account.
- Claude: Anthropic Messages API, raw HTTPS from Rust via the existing `reqwest` (there is no official Rust SDK).

## 4. Approaches considered (and what was picked)

| Decision | Options considered | Picked | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Live transcription | **Local Whisper** vs cloud streaming (Deepgram, word-by-word, ~£0.35/h) | Local Whisper (Maximilian) | Free, private, same engine as dictation; ~1–3 s lag after each sentence is fine. |
| Speaker names | **Read Teams' active-speaker highlight via accessibility** vs read Teams live captions vs voice diarization | Active speaker (Maximilian) | Works without turning captions on each meeting; Granola ships exactly this. |
| Starting | Auto-detect prompt vs **manual** | Manual (Maximilian) | He doesn't want the prompt. |
| Meeting audio | delete / **keep 24 h** / 3 days / forever | 24 h (Maximilian) | Lets him replay a line for a day. Transcript and notes are kept until he deletes the meeting. |
| Enhanced lengths | one fixed format vs **Short / Medium / Long** | S/M/L (Maximilian) | Different meetings need different depth. |
| System audio capture | **Teams-only process capture** vs whole-computer loopback | Teams-only, whole-computer as fallback (Claude's call) | Keeps YouTube/notification sounds out of the transcript; fallback keeps it working if the process capture fails. |
| Claude model | **Opus 5.5** vs Sonnet 5.5 | Opus 5.5 default, Sonnet 5.5 selectable (Claude's call) | ~$0.10–0.20 per hour-long meeting on Opus; the $100 monthly credit covers hundreds. |

## 5. How it works

### 5.1 Audio capture (two separate streams)

- **Me** = the microphone (the device chosen in Settings → Microphone, same as dictation).
- **Them** = Teams' audio output.
  - **Windows:** WASAPI *process loopback* (`ActivateAudioInterfaceAsync` with
    `AUDIOCLIENT_ACTIVATION_TYPE_PROCESS_LOOPBACK`, `PROCESS_LOOPBACK_MODE_INCLUDE_TARGET_PROCESS_TREE`) on the
    running `ms-teams.exe`. This follows Teams to whatever output device it uses. If Teams is running but
    process capture fails, fall back to loopback of the default output device and show
    "Capturing all computer sound" in the meeting header. (If Teams isn't running at all, see below.)
  - **macOS:** Core Audio *process tap* (`CATapDescription` + private aggregate device, macOS 14.2+) on the
    Teams process(es). Requires `NSAudioCaptureUsageDescription` in `Info.plist` and the "System Audio
    Recording" permission; Wren shows a one-time permission card like the existing Accessibility banner.
    Fallback: a global tap of all processes except Wren, with the same header note.
- Both streams are resampled to 16 kHz mono and written continuously to `<data>/meetings/<id>/me.wav` and
  `them.wav` (for replay), and fed to the segmenter.
- Dictation keeps working during a meeting (its own mic stream, unchanged). **While the dictation hotkey is
  held / hands-free is on, the meeting's "Me" segmenter is paused** (plus ~300 ms either side), so dictated
  text (e.g. a Teams chat message) never appears as something he said in the meeting. `session.rs` signals
  dictation start/stop to the meeting worker. (Claude's call.)
- If Teams isn't running when he clicks Start, the meeting still starts (mic only) and the header says
  "Teams isn't open — only your mic is being recorded"; Wren attaches to Teams when it appears.

### 5.2 Segmenting and transcribing

- Per stream, a voice-activity segmenter cuts speech into segments: end after ~700 ms of silence or at 20 s;
  ignore blips under ~400 ms. Thresholds are the builder's to tune (Granola's are a good start:
  RMS ≥ 0.01 on 32 ms hops, 800 ms silence, 25 s max).
- Each segment is written to a temp WAV and sent to the existing sidecar via `Engine::transcribe`, with bias
  terms = Wren dictionary terms + known participant names.
- **Dictation priority:** the meeting worker keeps **at most one** meeting request in flight, so a dictation
  never waits behind more than one meeting segment (~0.3 s on Windows, ~1 s on Mac). Segments queue in
  Rust, oldest first; if the queue backs up (> ~60 s of audio), the header shows "Catching up…".
- Segments are written to SQLite as soon as they're transcribed (crash-safe), then emitted to the UI.
- **While someone is talking**, the transcript shows a pending row for that stream ("Sarah Jones · …") from
  the segmenter's speech-active state; it fills in when the text arrives. (No interim word-by-word text.)
- **Bleed-through (speakers, no headphones):** drop a "Me" segment if it overlaps a "Them" segment in time
  and their texts are ≥ 80 % similar. Empty / hallucination-only results (the sidecar's VAD already filters
  silence) are discarded.

### 5.3 Speaker names from Teams

- **Me** lines are labelled with Maximilian's name (new setting "Your name in meetings", default
  "Maximilian").
- **His own Teams identity:** his Teams display name (work account) is likely different from "Maximilian".
  The watcher identifies his own roster entry (Teams marks it "(You)" / "Me") and (a) never assigns that
  name to a "Them" line, (b) leaves it out of the participant chips (he's shown once, as his meeting name),
  and (c) tells Claude that name is an alias of his meeting name so action items aimed at him land on him.
  The Teams name is stored per meeting, not shown in the UI. (Claude's call.)
- **Muted in Teams (Maximilian, 2026-11-24):** the watcher also reads his own mute state from Teams (the
  mic button / his roster entry). "Me" segments spoken while muted are stored with a `muted` flag, shown
  greyed with a "muted" tag in the transcript, and **left out of the enhanced-notes prompt**. If the mute
  state can't be read on an OS, all "Me" lines count as unmuted and this is reported, not hidden.
- **Them** lines get a name from Teams' **active speaker** indicator:
  - A watcher reads the Teams meeting window through UI Automation (Windows, existing
    `Win32_UI_Accessibility`) / the AX API (macOS, alongside `watcher/ax_macos.rs`) every ~250 ms and records
    a timeline of `(time, names currently marked as speaking)`, plus the participant roster and the meeting
    title (from the window title) when readable.
  - A "Them" segment takes the name with the most speaking-overlap within `[start − lag, end]`
    (lag ≈ 900 ms, Granola's Teams value; tunable). Confidence = overlap share.
  - No name or low confidence → label "Speaker" (shown greyed); two people talking at once → the one with
    more overlap.
  - Names can be fixed by hand: click a speaker label → rename this line, or every line with that label.
    Renames are saved and used in enhanced notes.
- **Unknown to be settled by the builder with a live probe:** where exactly new Teams exposes the speaking
  state in its accessibility tree on each OS (and whether macOS needs `AXManualAccessibility` /
  `AXEnhancedUserInterface` on the Teams WebView). Build a small probe tool that dumps the Teams window's tree,
  capture dumps during a real call as test fixtures, and keep the parser pure so it's unit-tested from those
  fixtures. If the speaking state can't be read on an OS, that OS falls back to "Speaker" labels with manual
  rename, and this is reported to Maximilian rather than hidden.

### 5.4 Enhanced notes (Claude)

- **When:** on Stop, **Medium** generates automatically. Short and Long generate the first time he picks them,
  then are cached. "Regenerate" replaces the version shown. Editing My notes or renaming speakers after
  generation shows "Notes changed — regenerate?" on the Enhanced tab.
- **Request:** `POST https://api.anthropic.com/v1/messages`, `stream: true`, model `claude-opus-5-5`
  (or `claude-sonnet-5-5` if chosen in Settings), adaptive thinking (default), `output_config.effort`
  `medium`, `max_tokens` 16000, server-side refusal fallback (`anthropic-beta: server-side-fallback-2026-07-01`,
  `fallbacks: "default"`). The text streams into the Enhanced tab as it's written.
- **Prompt layout (for caching):** fixed system prompt → first user block = meeting facts (title, date,
  duration, participants, his name), My notes, and the full named, timestamped transcript, marked
  `cache_control: ephemeral` → a final short block with the length instruction. Switching length within a
  few minutes reuses the cached transcript.
- **Content rules in the system prompt:** British English; plain, simple wording; use only what the
  transcript and his notes say; attribute statements and action items to the named speaker; write "someone"
  rather than guess when a speaker is "Speaker"; give his own notes priority as signals of what mattered.
- **Formats (markdown, fixed headings):**
  - **Short** (~half a page): 3–5 line summary, key points (≤ 8 bullets), action items.
  - **Medium** (~1 page, default): Summary; Topics discussed (heading per topic, bullets); Decisions;
    Action items (`Owner — task — due date if said`); Open questions.
  - **Long** (several pages): everything in Medium, plus each topic walked through in order with who said
    what and the reasoning, and any numbers/dates mentioned.
- **Footer** under each version, small and grey: "Claude Opus 5.5 · 18.2k tokens · ~$0.11 · 14:32".
- **API key:** Settings → new "Meeting notes" section: key field (paste → **Test key** → saved), model choice,
  "This month: ~$2.40 across 14 notes" (sum of recorded usage × list price). The key is stored in the OS
  credential store (Windows Credential Manager / macOS Keychain, e.g. the `keyring` crate), never in
  `settings.json`, the DB or logs, and never sent back to the frontend (only "key saved ✓ / remove").
- **Errors, shown on the Enhanced tab with a clear action:** no key → "Add your Claude API key in Settings";
  401 → "Key rejected"; 429/529/network → automatic retry with backoff, then a Retry button; refusal →
  "Claude declined to summarise this meeting" + Retry. Transcript and My notes are never affected.

### 5.5 Storage and retention

- SQLite (existing store, new migration):
  - `meetings` — id, title, started/ended times, duration, status (`recording | paused | ended |
    interrupted`), my-notes document, capture mode (Teams-only / all sound), audio_deleted_at.
  - `meeting_segments` — meeting id, source (`me|them`), speaker name, confidence, manual-rename flag,
    start/end ms, text, muted flag.
  - `meeting_notes` — meeting id, length (`short|medium|long`), markdown, model, input/output/cached tokens,
    estimated cost, created at, "stale" flag.
- Audio (`me.wav`, `them.wav`, temp segment WAVs) is deleted **24 h after the meeting ends**, by the
  existing retention sweep. Transcript, My notes and enhanced notes stay until he deletes the meeting
  (Delete, with confirm, removes everything).
- If Wren quits or crashes mid-meeting, on restart the meeting shows as **Interrupted** with everything
  transcribed so far, and enhanced notes can still be generated.
- Quitting Wren while recording asks "Stop meeting notes and quit?".

## 6. What he sees

### 6.1 Sidebar
New **Meetings** item (below Home), with a red dot while a meeting is recording.

### 6.2 Meetings page (list)

```
Meetings                                               [● Start meeting notes]
Today
  ┌ Weekly sync with Sarah            10:00 · 32 min · Sarah, Tom ─────────┐
  │ Rollout moved to Thursday; Tom chasing QA sign-off.                    │
  └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
Yesterday
  ┌ Supplier call                     15:30 · 48 min · 3 people ───────────┐
```
Day-grouped cards in the same style as History; first line of the Medium summary as the preview.

### 6.3 Meeting view

```
← Meetings
Weekly sync with Sarah ✎        ● Recording 32:14   Teams only   [Pause] [Stop]
Sarah Jones · Tom Reid · Maximilian

[ My notes ]  [ Transcript ]  [ Enhanced ]

Transcript                                                     ↓ Jump to live
  Sarah Jones    10:02   So the rollout moves to Thursday, assuming QA…
  Maximilian     10:02   Fine by me, as long as QA signs off.
  Tom Reid       10:03   I'll chase QA today.                        ▶
  Sarah Jones    · · ·
```

- Title is pre-filled from the Teams window title ("Weekly sync with Sarah | Microsoft Teams" → "Weekly sync
  with Sarah"), else "Meeting 24 Nov, 10:00"; editable.
- **During recording** the Transcript tab is open by default; after Stop the Enhanced tab opens.
- Transcript: consecutive lines from one speaker group into one run; each speaker gets a consistent accent
  colour; auto-scroll unless he's scrolled up ("↓ Jump to live"). For 24 h, hovering a line shows ▶ to
  play that stretch of the matching stream.
- My notes: a simple Notion-like editor (headings, bullets, checkboxes, bold), autosaved. Builder picks the
  library (TipTap is a fine choice).
- Enhanced: `Short | Medium | Long` segmented control, Regenerate, **Copy** (copies markdown and rich text),
  streaming text while generating, the footer from §5.4.

### 6.4 While Teams is in front
- Tray / menu-bar icon shows a recording state; its menu gets "Pause meeting notes" and "Stop meeting notes".
- Nothing takes focus from Teams.

## 7. Testing (definition of done)

- **Unit (Rust/TS):** segmenter; speaker attribution against fixture timelines; Teams accessibility-tree
  parser against captured dumps (both OSes); bleed-through dedupe; prompt builder; SSE stream parser
  (including error and refusal events); retention sweep; key never serialised.
- **Integration:** feed a recorded `me.wav` + `them.wav` pair through the full pipeline with the live engine
  (`WREN_LIVE=1`) on both OSes; dictation latency measured with a meeting running (must stay within one
  segment's transcription time of today's p50).
- **Live:** on this PC and on the MacBook (via a T3 thread on "Lord's MacBook Pro"): start/pause/stop,
  Teams-only capture, fallback capture, crash → Interrupted, 24 h audio deletion (time-shifted), all three
  enhanced lengths against the real API.
- **Needs Maximilian:** one real Teams call with at least one other person on each OS, to capture the
  accessibility fixtures and confirm names; pasting his Anthropic API key.
- **Council review** (regular: Claude Opus 5.5, GPT Sol, Gemini 4 Neon; effort high) for Windows + macOS
  bugs, fixes, re-review until they're satisfied.
- **Release:** "polished update to both apps" means shipped. Work happens on a feature branch (pushing to
  `main` auto-releases); after the council passes it merges to `main`, CI publishes the Windows and macOS
  builds, and both installed apps are updated through the in-app updater and re-checked live (the macOS
  System Audio Recording permission must survive the update). (Claude's call.)
